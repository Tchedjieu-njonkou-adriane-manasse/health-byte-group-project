Your medical record, in one place — and always yours to control.

HealthByte keeps one lifelong health record for every patient. Doctors can request access to add consultations, prescriptions and lab results — but only once you approve them. Every update is logged automatically, so there's always a clear, checkable history of what changed and when.

How it works

Step 1
Sign up as a patient or a doctor.
Step 2
A doctor requests access to your record — you choose to approve or decline.
Step 3
Every update is recorded and can be checked for accuracy at any time.

For patients

Your ID, history, allergies, consultations, prescriptions and lab results — all in one place, shared only with doctors you approve.

For doctors

Search any patient by ID or name, request access, and once approved, review their full history and log new consultations, prescriptions and lab results in seconds.

Always verifiable

Every change to a record is logged the moment it happens. Verify Integrity checks that history against what's stored, so you can always confirm nothing was quietly altered.
